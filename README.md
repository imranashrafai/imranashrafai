## About me

I build AI systems and then try to explain why they behave the way they do.

By day I'm an **Associate AI Engineer at Connectia Technologies** in Islamabad, working on LLM features, RAG and agent building, with a lot of plain backend work around them (NestJS, AWS, mobile push, auth). Since 2023 I've also been a **Research Assistant at VLCMatrixLab**, where I lead projects and mentor a small group of students.

My research sits in **explainable AI**. My main paper, on explainable phishing website detection, came out in *Scientific Reports* (Nature Portfolio) in 2025, and I was the corresponding author. In total I have **12 papers** across IEEE, Springer and Nature venues, covering interpretable fake news detection, explainable plant disease diagnosis, multi agent RL for vehicle platooning, and medical and surveillance vision.

I graduated in Software Engineering from the **University of Lahore as Gold Medalist** of my batch (CGPA 3.80). Next, I'm aiming for a **fully funded PhD in Europe** on explainable and agentic AI.

<br/>

## What I'm focused on

- 🔍 **Explainable AI**: models that show their reasoning in a form a domain expert can check
- 🤖 **Agentic AI**: LLM agents that plan, call tools, and fail in ways you can trace
- 🚗 **Multi agent RL**: cooperative driving with MAPPO and centralised training
- 👁️ **Computer vision**: detection and classification where a wrong answer has a cost
